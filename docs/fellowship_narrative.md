# Mozilla Fellowship Narrative - JIFUNZE AI
## The problem
Commercial educational AI extracts value and data from African schools: learner conversations
train foreign models, pricing excludes public schools, and vendors decide what a child's
assistant may say. Meanwhile, capable local educators have no sovereign tool of their own.
## The intervention
JIFUNZE AI is an open-source, community-governed AI learning infrastructure:
- Source-grounded answers from each school's own approved materials, with citations.
- Provider-independent AI gateway: hosted models or local inference; no vendor lock-in.
- Institutional data sovereignty: consent records, audit trail, export and deletion workflows.
- Built for low-bandwidth reality and self-hosting, so data can remain in school custody.
## Alignment with Mozilla's mission
- Digital sovereignty: schools own and control their educational data.
- Trustworthy AI: transparency statements, provider identification, honest uncertainty.
- Openness: Apache-2.0 code, CC-BY documentation, public CI enforcing tenant isolation.
- Community: contribution guidelines, code of conduct, planned governance council.
## Evidence today
- Public repository with complete MVP: Learning Companion, Teacher Studio, Sovereignty Centre.
- Automated security tests (tenant isolation, role enforcement, real deletion) running in CI.
- Published governance documents: architecture, AI transparency, data governance, privacy.
## Impact plan (12 months)
Phase 1: semantic retrieval (pgvector), real provider adapters including Ollama local bundle.
Phase 2: offline PWA and Swahili interface for low-connectivity schools.
Phase 3: guided pilots in 3 schools including one low-connectivity institution.
Phase 4: county deployment playbook, public model cards, community governance council.
## Why me
I build education systems for African schools daily. JIFUNZE AI converts that operational
experience into public-interest infrastructure: privacy-first for children, transparent
about AI, and owned by the communities it serves. The fellowship accelerates the journey
from working prototype to governed, piloted public good.
