# Privacy and Child Safeguarding
## Data minimisation
JIFUNZE collects no dates of birth, national IDs or addresses. Learner identity is a username
and a boolean is_minor flag - nothing more is required to learn.
## Children's data
- Designed around the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019) and comparable African frameworks.
- Guardian-authorised consent is supported for minors.
- Age-appropriate tone is enforced in learning prompts.
- No advertising, no profiling, no sale or sharing of learner data - ever.
## Safeguarding commitments
- Conversations are visible only to the learner and institution administrators under audited access.
- Uncertainty and refusal behaviour prevents harmful fabrication.
- Schools can export or delete a child's data at any time via the Sovereignty Centre.
## Deployment privacy
Schools may self-host JIFUNZE on local infrastructure so that data never leaves their custody.
Local inference (Ollama) removes external processing entirely where hardware allows.
