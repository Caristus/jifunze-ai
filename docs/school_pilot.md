# JIFUNZE AI School Pilot - One Pager
## What JIFUNZE offers your school
- AI learning assistant that answers only from materials your teachers approve, with citations.
- Teacher Studio that drafts lesson plans, quizzes and marking guides for CBC/CBE or Cambridge.
- Data Sovereignty Centre: your school's data stays yours - export or delete at any time, full audit trail.
## What we ask
- 2 volunteer teachers and 1 class for one term.
- 30-minute onboarding session; 20-minute feedback calls at mid-term and end-term.
- Permission to publish anonymised usage counts (never content, never learner identities).
## Data commitments
1. The school may self-host; otherwise data is stored under Kenyan jurisdiction.
2. No learner date of birth, national ID or address is ever collected.
3. Guardian-authorised consent for minors; withdrawal honoured immediately.
4. Export (JSON) and deletion workflows available to the school admin at all times.
## Timeline
Week 1: onboarding and resource upload workshop.
Weeks 2-8: classroom use; weekly check-in message.
Week 9: review session; co-authored pilot summary.
## Success measures
- Resources uploaded and chunked; questions answered with citations.
- Teacher preparation hours saved (self-reported).
- Zero tenant-isolation or privacy incidents (monitored via audit trail).
