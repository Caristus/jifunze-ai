Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants convert light energy into chemical energy. It occurs in chloroplasts, where chlorophyll absorbs mainly red and blue light. The overall equation is 6CO2 + 6H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6O2. Light-dependent reactions split water and produce ATP and NADPH; the Calvin cycle fixes carbon dioxide into glucose.
